YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy & analytics.

Obaid Web Lab can use Google Analytics to understand which parts of the learning library help visitors. Analytics is optional and starts only after you choose Allow analytics.

What we measure

After permission, we record page and section views, class or study-note opens, file type selections, ZIP/file downloads, editor actions such as running a preview or saving a local draft, search usage without the words searched, coarse scroll-depth milestones, and the number of search results. This helps us improve learning materials and the site experience.

What we do not send

We do not send the text typed into the library search, code typed into the browser editor, editor contents, file names, or form values to Google Analytics. We do not add names, email addresses, or personal identifiers to analytics events. The editor saves drafts in the visitor's own browser; those drafts are not uploaded to this website or GitHub.

What Google Analytics may receive

Google Analytics may process standard technical and usage information such as browser/device details, approximate location, visit and interaction metadata, and analytics identifiers when analytics is enabled. The Google tag is not loaded when analytics is declined. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in this site's tag configuration.

See Google's Privacy Policy ↗ for information about Google's processing.

Your choice

Your choice is stored in local storage on this device. Select decline to stop future analytics events and remove accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site. This cannot recall information already sent to Google. You can revisit your preference using the settings control below; clearing browser site data also resets the choice.
